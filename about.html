About me

I'm a third-year Business Computing student who likes building the whole thing: the database, the API, the interface, and the AI layer that makes it useful.

I study at ESB, Esprit School of Business in Tunis, where I'm in the Business Information Systems track with an average of 15/20 (Mention Bien). Programming, databases and web development are where I do my best work.

My Baccalauréat was in economics, and it still shows in what I build. Many of my projects deal with balance sheets, credit risk and tax data: tools that read financial statements, check the numbers and explain the result in plain words.

In 2026 my team TASHIL reached the final of the national hackathon on AI and public finance with SENTINELLE, a platform that helps tax inspectors decide which companies to audit first. Right now I'm completing a remote full-stack internship with Auspify Technologies and looking for my final-year internship, starting in February 2027.

Outside of code, I run a small streetwear brand, which taught me a lot about customers, content and shipping things on time.
